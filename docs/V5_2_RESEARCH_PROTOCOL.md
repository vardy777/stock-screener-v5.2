# V5.2 Research Protocol

Features for D may consume only facts available by the declared D-close
boundary. Labels consume D+1 through D+5 exchange sessions and remain
inaccessible to feature and ranking code. Historical universe membership must
include securities later delisted and dated ST, suspension and listing state.

Random train/test split is prohibited. Formal research uses preregistered,
contiguous walk-forward training, validation, locked test, forward and shadow
ranges. Test results cannot be reused for tuning.

Top 5/10/20/50 evaluation must include mean/median return, hit rate,
precision@K, benchmark excess, drawdown, MAE/MFE, turnover, capacity,
liquidity, sector concentration and regime stability. The final evidence may
say `ALPHA FOUND` or `ALPHA NOT FOUND`; Phase 0 produces neither verdict.
